Core Competencies

The Importance of Empowerment Technology

Empowerment Technology helps students improve their digital skills and become more knowledgeable about modern technology. Through this subject, we learn how to create presentations, design infographics, develop blogs, and use different online platforms. These skills help us complete our school activities and prepare for future careers.

Explore Practical Skills

Interactive Presentations

Mastering hyperlinking, custom animations, and slide design to deliver engaging digital lectures and reports.

Infographic Design

Transforming complex data into clear, visual stories using color theory, typography, and graphic layout tools.

Blogging & Web Publishing

Publishing articles, managing content management systems (CMS), and sharing insights with online audiences.

Online Platforms & Cloud Tools

Utilizing cloud drives, online forms, and collaborative spaces to work together in real-time anywhere.

Hardware & Systems Diagnostic

Basic computer troubleshooting, understanding system specs, software installation, and network setup.

Responsible Use

The Benefits of Technology & Digital Citizenship

Technology makes learning more convenient and accessible. Students can research information, collaborate with classmates, and share their ideas with people around the world. However, we must also be responsible digital citizens by protecting our privacy, checking the accuracy of information, and respecting others online.
